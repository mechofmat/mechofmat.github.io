    Travel


    The Fort Walton Beach airport (VPS) is  served by the airline companies:
                        - Delta/Air France
                        - American Airline
                        - United Airline

    From VPS airport, a taxi costs approximately 35$ (~20-25 min)





    Visa



    All French citizens entering the United States (by air or sea) are required to hold a valid ESTA travel authorization ( http://www.esta.us/). If you are from any other country, find out about visa requirements before you travel by visiting https://travel.state.gov/content/visas/en.html
